# Swing Trader and machine-learning paper experiment

## Swing research and updates

Swing Trader is a separately installed specialist. Trading Division includes swing recommendations, the next scheduled email, actual connected paper positions and a strategy comparison panel. Swing analysis uses completed daily closes, a prior 20-session breakout, SMA20 above SMA50, relative volume >=1 and a structural five-session-low stop. The next-session trigger is above the signal bar high with a 0.5% chase cap and 2R target. Long-only whole-share plans reserve modelled costs in the 0.25% risk budget. A fresh bid/ask and completed-minute confirmation are required before an entry condition is labelled met. Earnings/event coverage is explicitly unverified; never assume a complete event calendar.

The Alpaca session calendar determines open, midpoint and close+five-minute update times, including holidays and early closes. Normal Toronto times: 09:30, 12:45 and 16:05. The worker checks every 30 seconds while ADRIAN/PC/network are running. A 15-minute delivery window prevents stale catch-up messages; missed/failed slots are journalled. SMTP acceptance is not inbox-delivery proof. Daily signals conservatively use the prior completed session because daily bars can include extended-hours trades. Closing snapshots and positions are separately timestamped. Swing recommendations are advisory and do not submit orders.

## Strategy creation and improvement

The lab tests a bounded, inspectable family of daily breakout strategies. Lookback, structural stop length, maximum holding sessions, volume filter and reward/risk can change. It uses common date boundaries for all symbols: 60% earlier training, 20% validation and 20% later test. Candidate selection occurs on training only; later-period comparisons and cost scenarios are reported after the candidate is frozen. Trades enter on the next session trigger without chasing gaps, use whole-share 0.25% risk sizing and cash caps, and pessimistically assume stops first when daily price paths are ambiguous.

Results include exact changed rules, per-symbol independent-account return, costs, trade count, win rate, expectancy, closed-trade drawdown, baseline comparisons and benchmark. Independent $2,000 accounts are not a combined portfolio. Segment-end positions are force-closed. Changed universes have survivorship/selection bias. Reused overlapping test periods are labelled reused, not untouched evidence. No backtest automatically activates a strategy or guarantees income.

## Real machine learning

The separate one-week day-trading paper worker learns ten causal price/volume/trend features using scikit-learn RandomForestClassifier. Forty trees and three bounded depth/leaf alternatives are selected by chronological validation Brier error. Labels measure same-session next-open to fifth subsequent 15-minute close net return after a fixed 40-bps cost assumption. Training labels finish before validation starts; validation labels finish before later test begins. Historical session calendars exclude extended hours and early-close periods. Classification scores are uncalibrated and not guaranteed profitability probabilities. Predictive label returns are not bracket-order backtest results.

Each saved model includes feature importance, sample counts, chronological dates, error metrics and nonoverlapping signal diagnostics. Retraining runs daily on newly matured history, records rolling-window comparisons and flags reused test data. Frozen chosen parameters are refit on matured history only after evaluation. A second learner uses actual settled broker entry/exit outcomes minus a stated cost reserve after at least 40 trades and sufficient chronological winners/losers. If it beats its constant baseline, it can add an extra entry filter; it cannot enlarge risk. A handful of trades is not enough to establish a reliable edge.

## Authorized automatic paper orders

The experiment must be explicitly started. The connected account is Alpaca PAPER, not real money. It runs seven calendar days, regular stock sessions only. No crypto/24-hour order flow or live endpoint is present. New entry scans run every five minutes; order monitoring and risk checks every 30 seconds. Later-period model diagnostics must pass experimental eligibility before an entry. Trade requirements include score >=0.65, trend/volume checks, fresh quotes, a completed minute, small spread and a bounded chase. Failed evidence produces NO TRADE, potentially for the entire experiment.

Hard limits: 0.25% equity risk per trade including assumed costs, 1% daily loss headroom, 2% total experiment headroom, three symbols maximum, whole shares and cash-only sizing. Risk of existing bot entries/positions and cash reserved by pending buys count against new orders. Equity includes any other account activity, so avoid manually trading this shared experiment account. Limits cannot guarantee a maximum loss during gaps or delayed execution.

Actual orders use the fixed `https://paper-api.alpaca.markets` endpoint, unique persisted client IDs and day limit-entry brackets. No fill is claimed from order acceptance. Unknown submission outcomes block further entries and are reconciled instead of blindly retried. Unfilled entries are cancelled after three minutes; broker-managed stop/target legs protect fills. The worker requests a time exit after 75 minutes, before session close, at the experiment deadline, or on a loss stop. It cancels only bot-owned orders, verifies cancellation before market exits, checks actual remaining long quantity and refuses to flatten additional manual shares. Exit uncertainty requires broker reconciliation/manual review. Keep ADRIAN running: broker brackets persist, but software time exits do not run while the PC/server is off.

Open/midday/close experiment emails show model diagnostics, actual order journal and account change from starting paper equity. The Manager daily report includes swing and experiment work. Final profit is not guaranteed; losses, no-trade days, failures and insufficient samples are reported honestly. All private credentials and generated model/order journals remain in the local database and are excluded from GitHub.
